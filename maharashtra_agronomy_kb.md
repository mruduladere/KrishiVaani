# Maharashtra Agronomy & Plant Health Knowledge Base
*Verified Agronomic Advisories, Crop Health Diagnostic Keys, and Open-Access Standards for Maharashtra State*

---

## 1. Open-Access Portals & Agricultural Authorities

### A. CROPSAP Maharashtra (Crop Pest Surveillance and Advisory Project)
- **Joint Initiative:** Commissionerate of Agriculture (Govt. of Maharashtra) & ICAR - National Research Centre for Integrated Pest Management (ICAR-NCIPM / NRIIPM, New Delhi).
- **Coverage:** Active across 28–34 agrarian districts covering 174+ lakh hectares.
- **Focus Field Crops:** Cotton (*कापूस*), Soybean (*सोयाबीन*), Paddy/Rice (*भात/धान*), Tur/Pigeon Pea (*तूर*), Gram/Chickpea (*हरभरा*), Maize (*मका*), Sorghum/Jowar (*ज्वारी*), Sugarcane (*ऊस*).
- **Core Principle:** Pest and disease interventions MUST be driven by **Economic Threshold Levels (ETL)** rather than calendar-based preventative spraying.

### B. HORTSAP Maharashtra (Horticultural Pest Surveillance and Advisory Project)
- **Authority:** State Department of Horticulture & State Agricultural Universities.
- **Coverage:** 32 districts across 241 horticulture blocks.
- **Focus Horticultural Crops:** Mango (*आंबा*), Sapota/Chiku (*चीकू*), Sweet Orange/Mosambi (*मोसंबी*), Nagpur Mandarin/Santra (*संत्रा*), Pomegranate (*डाळिंब*), Banana (*केळी*), Tomato (*टोमॅटो*), Okra/Bhindi (*भेंडी*).
- **Core Principle:** Integrated Pest Management (IPM), micro-irrigation fertigation guidelines, and seasonal weather-based disease forecasting.

### C. State Agricultural Universities (SAUs) & ICAR Research Institutes
- **MPKV Rahuri (Mahatma Phule Krishi Vidyapeeth):** Western Maharashtra (Ahmednagar, Pune, Solapur, Nashik, Dhule, Jalgaon, Nandurbar, Satara, Kolhapur, Sangli). Specializes in sugarcane, onion, pomegranate, grapes, pulses, and micronutrient formulations ("Phule Micro Grade").
- **Dr. PDKV Akola (Dr. Panjabrao Deshmukh Krishi Vidyapeeth):** Vidarbha region (Akola, Amravati, Buldhana, Yavatmal, Washim, Wardha, Nagpur, Bhandara, Gondia, Chandrapur, Gadchiroli). Specializes in rainfed cotton, soybean, red gram, and citrus/Nagpur mandarin.
- **VNMKV Parbhani (Vasantrao Naik Marathwada Krishi Vidyapeeth):** Marathwada region (Parbhani, Aurangabad/Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar, Nanded, Beed, Jalna, Latur, Osmanabad/Dharashiv, Hingoli). Specializes in cotton, soybean, jowar, sweet orange (mosambi), and safflower.
- **Dr. BSKKV Dapoli (Dr. Balasaheb Sawant Konkan Krishi Vidyapeeth):** Coastal Konkan (Thane, Palghar, Raigad, Ratnagiri, Sindhudurg). Specializes in Alphonso mango, cashew, coconut, arecanut, and coastal lowland paddy.
- **ICAR-NRCG (National Research Centre for Grapes, Pune):** National authority on export-grade table grapes, downy mildew forecasting, berry cracking, and residue-free IPM.
- **ICAR-DOGR (Directorate of Onion and Garlic Research, Rajgurunagar, Pune):** National authority on rabi/kharif/rangada onion diseases (purple blotch, thrips, Stemphylium).

### D. Soil Health Card (SHC) Priority Rule
> **PRIMARY RULE OF FERTILIZER PRESCRIPTION:**
> Farm-specific Soil Health Card test parameters (**Available N, P, K, S, Zn, Fe, Cu, Mn, B, pH, EC, and Organic Carbon**) ALWAYS supersede and override generic agro-climatic zone package of practices. If an individual farm tests deficient in Zinc or Potassium, targeted foliar or basal corrective doses must be applied irrespective of general block-level recommendations.

## 2. Strict Diagnostic Differentiation Keys (Preventing Misdiagnosis)

To avoid catastrophic misidentification between nutritional deficiencies, abiotic stresses, fungal/bacterial blights, and insect pest damage, the following strict diagnostic boundaries must be observed:

| Visual Symptom Pattern | Affected Leaf Position | Leaf Morphology & Markings | Primary Diagnosis | Differential Diagnosis (Rule Out) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Marginal Scorch / Burnt Edges** | **Older / Lower leaves** | Yellowing begins at leaf tips and margins, progressing to necrotic brown scorching along borders while center remains green. | **Potassium (K) Deficiency** (*पोटॅशची कमतरता*) | Rule out Salinity/Sodium toxicity (EC test needed) and moisture stress wilt. |
| **Interveinal Chlorosis + Stunting** | **Younger / Upper / New leaves** | Veins remain distinctively green while tissue between turns pale yellow to bleached white; stunted leaves with shortened internodes (**"Little Leaf / Rosetting"**). | **Zinc (Zn) Deficiency** (*जस्त/झिंक कमतरता*) | Rule out Iron deficiency (Fe shows overall interveinal bleaching without severe little-leaf rosetting). |
| **Uniform Pale Yellowing** | **Older / Lower leaves** | Complete leaf blade turns light green to uniform pale yellow, starting from the leaf apex/tip inward along the midrib. | **Nitrogen (N) Deficiency** (*नायट्रोजन/नत्र कमतरता*) | Rule out Sulfur deficiency (Sulfur yellowing starts on **younger, upper leaves** first). |
| **Interveinal Purpling / Reddening** | **Older / Lower leaves** | Interveinal yellowing transitioning into deep bronze or purplish-red discoloration, with prominent green veins intact (common in Vidarbha/Marathwada Bt Cotton). | **Magnesium (Mg) Deficiency** (*मॅग्नेशियम कमतरता / लाल्या रोग*) | Rule out Jassid burn (jassids cause hopper burn curling downwards with triangular yellow-to-brown edge necrosis). |
| **Interveinal Whitening / Bleaching** | **Youngest Top / Terminal leaves** | Bright ivory to lemon-yellow chlorosis on newly emerging shoots in high-pH calcareous black soils (*चुनखडीयुक्त काळी जमीन*). | **Iron (Fe) Deficiency** (*लोहाची कमतरता*) | Rule out Zinc deficiency (Fe shows no rosetting/reduction in leaf blade surface area). |
| **Fruit Cracking & Shoot Dieback** | **Growing tips, blossoms & fruit** | Terminal buds die back, hollow stems, blossom drop, severe fruit cracking, and internal brown corky tissue (Pomegranate & Mango). | **Boron (B) Deficiency** (*बोरोनची कमतरता*) | Rule out Sudden irrigation fluctuation after dry spell (which causes longitudinal rind splitting without internal corkiness). |
| **Upward / Downward Leaf Curling with Silvering** | **Tender terminal shoots & leaves** | Boat-shaped upward cupping with silvery streaks underneath; or downward cupping with fine silken webs under the leaf blade. | **Sucking Pest Attack (Thrips / Mites)** (*फुलकिडे / कोळी प्रादुर्भाव*) | Rule out Viral Leaf Curl (Thrips/mites leaves feel thin and show mechanical rasping scratches; viral curl leaves are thickened, leathery, and brittle). |
| **Thick, Crinkled, Leathery Puckering** | **New growth & whole plant canopy** | Upward curling with severe vein thickening, enations (leaf-like outgrowths), yellow vein clearing, and extreme internode stunting. | **Viral Leaf Curl Complex (transmitted by Whitefly)** (*पाने आकसणे / विषाणूजन्य पर्णगुच्छ*) | Rule out Herbicide drift (2,4-D causes strap-like shoestringing without vein clearing). |

## 3. Verified Maharashtra Dosages & Package of Practices

### A. Cotton (*कापूस*) — Vidarbha & Marathwada
- **Sucking Pests (Aphids, Jassids, Thrips at ETL):**
  - Organic/Bio-control: 5% Neem Seed Kernel Extract (NSKE) or Azadirachtin 0.03% (300 ppm) @ 50 mL per 10 L water; or Azadirachtin 1500 ppm @ 25 mL per 10 L water.
  - ETL threshold: 10 nymphs of jassids or 10 thrips/aphids per leaf on 20 randomly inspected plants.
  - **ETL Safety Rule:** If predator populations (ladybird beetles, chrysoperla, syrphid fly larvae) exceed 1 per plant, defer chemical application.
- **Reddening / Lalya (*लाल्या व्यवस्थापन*):**
  - Magnesium Sulphate (MgSO4) 1% (100 g / 10 L water) + 19:19:19 @ 1% foliar spray at peak square and flowering stages (Dr. PDKV / VNMKV).

### B. Soybean (*सोयाबीन*) — Marathwada & Western Maharashtra
- **Zinc Deficiency & Chlorosis:**
  - Chelated Zinc (Zn-EDTA 12%) @ 1.5–2.0 g per Liter of water foliar spray at ~30–35 days after sowing (DAS).
- **Drought / Moisture Stress Mitigation:**
  - Foliar spray of 2% Potassium Nitrate (13:0:45) @ 200 g / 10 L water OR 2% Urea (200 g / 10 L) to sustain stomatal regulation during 10–15 day dry spells (MPKV / VNMKV).

### C. Sugarcane (*ऊस*) — Western Maharashtra (MPKV Rahuri)
- **Water Stress & Potassium Starvation:**
  - Combined foliar spray of 2% MOP (Muriate of Potash, 0:0:60) + 2% Urea @ 200 g each per 10 L water every 21 days during summer/water scarcity periods.
- **Iron Chlorosis on Calcareous Soils:**
  - Ferrous Sulphate (FeSO4) @ 0.5% (50 g / 10 L) + Citric Acid (10 g / 10 L) foliar spray 2–3 times at 10-day intervals.

### D. Onion (*कांदा*) — Nashik, Pune, Ahmednagar (ICAR-DOGR & MPKV)
- **Micronutrient Correction:**
  - Spray **Phule Micro Grade-II 'B'** @ 0.3% (3 g / L water) at 35 and 55 days after transplanting (DAT) for shallow-to-medium black soils, or at 55–65 DAP for bulbing stage.
- **Purple Blotch (*जांभळा करपा*) & Thrips Complex:**
  - Azadirachtin 1500 ppm @ 25 mL / 10 L water combined with sticker (spreader) @ 0.5 mL / L.
  - Safe bio-fungicide: *Trichoderma viride* or *Pseudomonas fluorescens* @ 5 g / L water.

### E. Maize & Wheat (*मका व गहू*)
- **Maize Micronutrient Deficiency:**
  - Phule Liquid Micronutrient Grade-II @ 1% foliar spray at knee-high stage (30–35 DAS).
- **Wheat False Smut & Leaf Blight:**
  - Copper Hydroxide 77% WP @ 1.0–1.5 g / L water; maintain balanced irrigation.

### F. Grapes (*द्राक्ष*) — ICAR-NRCG Pune / Nashik / Sangli
- **Downy Mildew & Powdery Mildew:**
  - Biological prophylaxis: *Bacillus subtilis* or *Ampelomyces quisqualis* @ 3–5 g / L.
  - Canopy management: Ensure open shoots and sunlight penetration. Never spray chemical protectants during open bloom to safeguard pollinator bees.

### G. Pomegranate (*डाळिंब*) — Solapur / Sangli / Nashik
- **Bacterial Blight (*तेल्या रोग - Xanthomonas axonopodis pv. punicae*):**
  - **CRITICAL MPKV / ICAR-NRCP CAUTION:** Do NOT rely on indiscriminate chemical sprays to cure internal systemic blight. Rigorous orchard sanitation, surgical removal of infected cankers with 1% Bordeaux paste, and copper bactericide barrier sprays are essential.
  - Boron Deficiency: Spray Solubor (Di-Sodium Octaborate Tetrahydrate) 20% @ 1.0–1.5 g / L water during pre-bloom and fruit set to prevent internal fruit cracking.

### H. Mango (*आंबा*) — Konkan & Western Maharashtra (Dr. BSKKV Dapoli)
- **Powdery Mildew (*भुरी*) & Blossom Midge:**
  - Spray wettable sulfur 80% WP @ 2.0 g / L water at bud emergence stage.
  - Boron application: Solubor @ 1 g / L during marble-sized fruit stage to control black tip and spongy tissue.

### I. Tomato (*टोमॅटो*) — Nashik, Pune, Ahmednagar, Satara (MPKV Rahuri & HORTSAP)
- **Strict Differentiation of Tomato Foliar Spot & Blight Complex:**
  - **Bacterial Spot (*जिवाणूजन्य ठिपके - Xanthomonas vesicatoria / X. perforans*):** Small (2–3 mm), dark brown-to-black angular spots that appear water-soaked/greasy on the underside, often with a faint chlorotic halo. Fruit shows small, raised dark scabby blisters. **Foliar/fruit surface pathogen.**
  - **Bacterial Canker (*बॅक्टेरियल कॅन्कर - Clavibacter michiganensis subsp. michiganensis*):** **Systemic vascular bacterial disease (distinct from Xanthomonas spot).** Lower leaflets curl upward, wilt unilaterally; leaf margins dry up and brown ("marginal firing"); stems exhibit internal vascular browning, pith decay, and open longitudinal cankers; fruit develops small white haloed spots with dark raised centers ("bird's-eye spots" / *पक्ष्यांच्या डोळ्यांसारखे डाग*).
  - **Early Blight (*लवकर येणारा करपा - Alternaria solani*):** Dark brown to black circular or oval spots characterized by distinct concentric rings (**"target-board" / "bull's-eye" pattern**), starting on older lower foliage, surrounded by a prominent yellow chlorotic halo.
  - **Late Blight (*उशिरा येणारा करपा - Phytophthora infestans*):** Large, irregular water-soaked greasy grayish-green lesions rapidly turning dark brown/black under cool, humid weather (>85% RH, 15–22°C), with delicate white fungal down visible on the underside during moist mornings.
  - **Potassium Deficiency in Tomato:** Chlorosis and necrosis strictly along leaf margins of older leaves; no concentric rings, no water-soaking, no fruit blisters.
- **Diagnostic Safeguard from Single Photographs:**
  - A photograph of tomato foliar spots is **presumptive, never definitive**. Do NOT assert a single pathogen without field verification of:
    1. Concentric rings (Early Blight).
    2. Water-soaked greasy underside (Bacterial Spot).
    3. Stem vascular discoloration, cankers, or bird's-eye fruit spots (Bacterial Canker).
- **Treatment Protocols & Safe Agronomy Rules:**
  - **Cultural & Preventive First:** Remove and burn infected lower leaves; avoid overhead sprinkler irrigation to minimize leaf wetness; sanitize pruning shears; practice crop rotation with non-solanaceous crops for 2–3 seasons.
  - **Chemical Safety Rule:** Never recommend synthetic fungicides or bactericides solely from an unconfirmed photograph. If bacterial or fungal blight is suspected, mandate: *"Inspect field symptoms for concentric rings or water-soaking, and verify exact registered molecules and dosage locally with Taluka Krishi Adhikari / Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK)."*

## 4. Universal Anti-Hallucination Guardrails & Local Consultation Mandates

1. **Exact Dosage Verification:**
   - For any crop-specific synthetic chemical pesticide or specialized combination not explicitly documented above with university verification, KrishiVaani strictly REFUSES to hallucinate exact chemical quantities.
   - It outputs:
     - **English:** *"Confirm exact dosage locally with Taluka Krishi Adhikari / Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK)."*
     - **Marathi:** *"स्थानिक तालुका कृषी अधिकारी / कृषी विज्ञान केंद्र (KVK) यांच्याकडून अधिकृत मात्रा तपासून घ्या."*
2. **Soil Health Card Priority:**
   - Generic fertilizer charts are only baselines. Whenever the farmer provides specific Soil Health Card parameters, recommendations must align with their specific nitrogen, phosphorus, potassium, and micronutrient indices.
3. **Pesticide Incompatibility Warning:**
   - Never mix alkaline compounds (like lime or copper sulfate) with organophosphates, synthetic pyrethroids, or microbial biopesticides.
4. **Beneficial Predator Safeguard:**
   - Always verify if the pest population has crossed the Economic Threshold Level (ETL) before advising systemic chemical interventions.
